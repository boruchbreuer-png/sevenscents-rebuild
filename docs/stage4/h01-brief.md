# H-01 — Handover Home Plate · Asset Brief
**Status: BRIEF ONLY — awaiting approval before any generation.** Governed by `00-brand-dna.md`, `project-checkpoint.md`. Third canon asset gate, after M-00 and P-11.

## 1 · Purpose
H-01 is **the arrival**: the plate the impossible transition resolves into. The visitor performs the First Cut, falls through the crumb, passes through the window — and lands *here*: **their own morning**. It replaces the canvas-placeholder room in the continuity scene and becomes the visual proof of the brand thesis: *the loaf handed the morning back to you.*

It is the emotional exhale after the film's climax. Everything about it is calm, resolved, and quiet — the opposite of a crescendo.

Canonical constraint from the approved transition review (`01a-transition-review.md`): **the home stays implied and universal** — the loaf and linen sharp, the room dissolved in light — never a specific styled set that reads as someone else's house or a stock lifestyle shot.

## 2 · Frame description
A quiet table in a lived-in, contemporary-timeless home, full morning. On it:
- **The same loaf — now CUT** (canon: the cut happened at 7:42; two or three slices lie beside the loaf, crumb face visible but not macro-hero'd). Present, modest, *not styled*.
- **A cup going cool** — ceramic, half-full, at most a thread of steam. Not a latte, no latte art.
- **A fold of ordinary linen** — soft, unposed.

The loaf sits **centre-low** (lower third). The room behind dissolves into warm linen light and soft focus — a wall, maybe the ghost of a chair or shelf edge, nothing legible enough to date or place it. Generous **warm negative space** in the upper half (the promise line and reserve UI will later live there). Composition law applies: silence margins, one subject, three-quarters quiet.

## 3 · Camera / lens
- **~50mm, table height** — the same lens family as M-00 (the continuity camera arrives at the table, so the plate is seen from where the visitor "lands": seated at their own table).
- Medium-shallow depth of field: **loaf, slices, linen, cup sharp; the room soft**.
- Straight-on to slightly low, no tilt, no drama. 16:9.

## 4 · Light direction
- **One source**: morning window light from the **upper-left, off-frame or barely implied** — we deliberately do *not* feature a second prominent window (the window we passed through is behind the camera; two hero windows would confuse the match-cut).
- Hour: **7:45–8:04** — clean linen morning, 3800–4200K. Palette: linen/cream/mist highlights, wheat mids, umber softs. No pure white, no pure black.
- Soft long-ish shadows falling right; faint dust motes in the light allowed; the same film stock: fine grain, gentle halation, lifted blacks, single-source vignette.

## 5 · Required canon elements
1. The **cut loaf** with visible score remnant on the crown — same boule family as P-11 (round, blistered, flour-dusted).
2. **Cup going cool** (ceramic, imperfect glaze, cream).
3. **Fold of unbleached linen**.
4. **Warm negative space** (upper half) for overlays.
5. **Single-source light**, same stock, same grade family as M-00/P-11.
6. Wood table — lived-in, warm, but a *home* table, not the bakehouse worktable (no cutting valley, no flour drifts — this room bakes nothing; it receives).

## 6 · What must be excluded
- **No people, hands, fingers, silhouettes, reflections of people.**
- **No bakehouse elements**: no oven, no 1931 lintel, no tally wall, no Mother jar, no peel — those live in the other room; their presence would collapse the two spaces.
- No café/coffee-shop signals, no food-magazine or advertisement styling, no rustic props (wheat stalks, scattered grain, boards-as-props, knife-as-prop, butter dish, jam jars, honey drizzle).
- No breakfast spread — this is not a meal; it is a morning.
- No visible text, branding, logos. No second light source. No cool tones except trace shadow neutrality.
- Nothing trendy, datable, or placeable (no Scandi styling, no farmhouse, no loft).

## 8 · Judging rubric
1. **Same production** — reads as the same film stock, grade, and light discipline as M-00/P-11; the loaf reads as *the same loaf*, now cut.
2. **The Handover emotion** — calm, resolved, private; "the loaf handed the morning back to you"; the exhale, not a crescendo.
3. **RITUAL without the loaf** — remove the loaf mentally: single-source linen light + linen fold + quiet composition must still carry the brand.
4. **Implied and universal room** — dissolved in light, undatable, unplaceable; fails if it reads as a styled set, a café, or anyone's specific house.
5. **One dominant light source**, upper-left, no competing window-hero.
6. **Canon elements present**: cut loaf w/ score remnant, cooling cup, linen fold, warm upper negative space.
7. **Zero forbidden signals** (people/props/styling/ads/rustic/café).
8. **Usable as the arrival plate**: loaf centre-low, upper half clean for overlays, composition survives a slow push-in landing.
