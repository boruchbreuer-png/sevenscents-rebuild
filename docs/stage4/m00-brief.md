## The prompt (v1)
> Cinematic film still, single frame from a feature film. An old stone-and-oak bakehouse interior that could be 1924 or 2094 — no era props, no country signifiers, no decoration. One deep-set window high on the left wall pours a single volumetric shaft of early-morning light through drifting flour motes; everything outside the beam falls into warm near-black shadow with lifted blacks, single-source chiaroscuro like Caravaggio and Vermeer, Roger Deakins interior lighting. On a massive old waxed-oak worktable: one round sourdough boule with a single curved score, resting beside a shallow valley worn into the wood by decades of cutting, faint steam rising through the beam. Half-lost in shadow behind: a lime-plastered wall covered in thousands of small pencil tally marks in groups of five fading with age, one fresh dark graphite mark; a carved stone lintel above a dark oven mouth bearing the chiseled numerals 1931; on a stone shelf, a glass starter jar wearing layered strata of old paper labels. A fold of unbleached linen, a cream ceramic bowl with imperfect glaze, matte black steel peel, brass fittings worn bright by hands. Palette: charcoal and umber shadows, wheat-gold beam, linen highlights; no pure black, no pure white. 35mm film photography, 50mm lens, medium-shallow depth of field, fine film grain, gentle highlight halation, single-source vignette. Composition: table and loaf in the lower third, the beam as the near-vertical axis, three-quarters of the frame in quiet shadow, enormous restrained negative space. Mood: silent, reverent, patient; luxury through restraint. Absolutely not: cheerful café, rustic Pinterest bakery, food-magazine styling, bright airy daylight, pastry display, people, text or signage other than the carved 1931, clutter.
