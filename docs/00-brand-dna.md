# PROJECT: RITUAL — Brand DNA
**The Constitution.** Permanent. Every stage, asset, line of copy, and pixel must comply with this document. When any later work conflicts with a fact, rule, or motif here, this document wins. Nothing in here changes without an explicit amendment.

---

## I · THE BELIEF (immutable)

**RITUAL is not selling bread. RITUAL is protecting mornings.**

The product is the last quiet hour of the day. Bread is the instrument by which it is kept. Every decision passes one test: **does this protect the hour, or does it steal from it?**

### The Creed
1. The day is decided in its first hour.
2. The first hour belongs to no one but you. The world may have the rest.
3. Slowness is not the absence of speed. It is the presence of attention.
4. The things that cannot be rushed are the things worth waking for.
5. A morning without ritual is just an alarm.
6. Bread is how we practice.

### The Promise (the brand's last words, everywhere)
> **Tomorrow is already proving.**

## III · THE NAMES

- **RITUAL** — the brand. Names what we protect. On the door.
- **The Mother** — the starter. Always capitalized. Referred to as *she*.
- **The Keeper** — the baker. Not a craftsman; the one who keeps the hour.
- **FOUR THIRTY** — the sacred number, not a sub-brand. Woven through, never on the logo.
- **A protected morning** — a reservation. Never "an order," never "a purchase."
- **A standing morning** — a subscription. Never "a plan," never "weekly delivery."

## IV · SYMBOLS & ICONOGRAPHY (the permanent marks)

These exist on packaging, signage, the door, and the bread before any screen.

1. **The Score** — *master mark.* One confident curved cut, the arc a lame leaves in a boule. Cuts through the wordmark; embossed on boxes; carved on the door; scored into the actual bread. **The only logo that can be baked.** Product and brand wear the same signature.
2. **The Tally** — groups-of-five strokes; the count of kept mornings. Packaging interiors, receipts, the wall. A year of standing mornings prints your year's tally inside the bag.
3. **The Arc of Hours** — a circle (loaf, clock, table) with the 4:12–8:04 span marked as an arc. The badge of the protected hour: stamp, wax-press, favicon.
4. **The Crumb** — irregular honeycomb from a real crumb scan. Blind emboss, tissue print, section texture. **Never a perfect hexagon grid** — always real, imperfect cells.
5. **The Object Lexicon** — the brand's *only* icons, drawn in one fixed line weight: **the Jar, the Lame, the Peel, the Linen fold, the Knife.** Generic iconography (carts, hearts, hamburgers, chevrons) never appears; these five and the marks above do all the work.

### Geometry law
The **circle** (loaf, clock, table) and the **arc** (score, sunrise) are the only permitted curves. The **vertical beam** (window light) is the only permitted straight emphasis. Every layout, dieline, and UI element composes from **circle + arc + beam**.

## V · RECURRING MOTIFS (the leitmotifs)

Threaded through every scene, asset, and page so the world is instantly recognizable:

- **The single light source.** One window; in Act II, the oven. Never flat, never top-lit. Light always comes from one place and has a temperature.
- **The count that never resets.** Bake №, mornings protected, your visit count — numbers that only ever climb. The brand ages visibly; so does the site.
- **Patina as proof.** Worn brass, the table's cut-valley, faded tally marks, label strata on the jar. Age is the argument.
- **Stillness rewarded.** The Proofing principle: what holds still is what develops. Applies to the interface and the tone alike.
- **The withheld bite.** The payoff is always one step away — the crackle before the cut, the butter before the bite, the reservation before the morning. Craving is never fully resolved on screen.
- **Time as the medium.** Scroll is time; color is time of day; scarcity is time (36 hours). The brand's material is time, not flour.

## VI · THE RITUALS (customer-facing ceremonies)

Ordinary transactions, re-authored as rites:

- **The Reservation → Protecting a Morning.** *"17 of 40 mornings protected."* → *"Your morning is protected. Loaf 17 bakes at 4:30."*
- **Scoring your loaf.** At reservation you draw your own score onto your loaf. *"Loaf 17 of 40. Scored by you."* The brand's master symbol, made by the customer.
- **The Standing Morning.** *"A protected morning, every Saturday."* Membership in the keeping, not a delivery schedule.
- **The Greeting.** Returning visitors are recognized, not re-onboarded. *"Your third morning here." … "The usual?"*
- **The Feeding.** Story ritual: the Mother is fed at six, before anyone eats. The daily proof the belief is practiced.

## VII · VOICE (immutable rules)

**Personality:** the quiet keeper — Aesop × a mechanical watchmaker × a Kyoto knife smith.

**Do:** short declarative sentences · present tense · sensory verbs · numbers with reverence · silence between lines.

**Never:** exclamation marks · the words *premium / luxury / artisanal / handcrafted* · puns · carb jokes · urgency-marketing ("Hurry! Limited!") · adjectives where a fact will do.

**Calibration:**
- "The Mother is fed at six. She eats before we do."
- "Forty loaves. Then the oven rests."
- "4:12. The world will wake soon. Not yet."
- "We have been awake since 4:30. You didn't have to be. That's the arrangement."
- "Protect tomorrow morning. It's already rising."

---

## VIII · SENSORY & EMOTIONAL KEYS

**Emotional keys:** anticipation · stillness · warmth · belonging · desire — rising to **conviction** (joining the keeping).
**Sensory keys:** crackle · steam · wheat · butter · morning light.
**Palette arc (time of day, not "brand colors"):** charcoal night → ember amber → wheat gold → linen cream → butter → honey. *(Exact values: Stage 2.)*
**Materials:** honed stone · dark waxed oak · unbleached linen · imperfect cream ceramics · matte black steel · hand-worn brass.
**Camera:** slow dolly/crane, never handheld · 100mm macro for crust/crumb/butter/steam · 35mm for room/hands · shallow DoF · film grain · warm grade, lifted blacks. One production, one morning, one lens kit.

---

## IX · THE ANTI-BRIEF (what RITUAL is not)

Not French, not Italian, not Scandinavian, not trendy — **timeless.** Not a bakery website; a belief system with a bakehouse attached. Not ecommerce; a ritual with a reservation. Not "beautiful and fast"; **slow on purpose.** No stock, no placeholders, no template, no cart, no hero-carousel, no "Shop Now." If a decision would fit any other bakery, it is wrong.
